/ COLOPHON · HIDDEN

how this site is made.

plain html, css and javascript, written by hand. no frameworks, no build step, no analytics, no ads.

// the pieces

hosting
github pages, at donidonka.info. the code is public: DoniDonka/officialwebsite.
font
IBM Plex Mono, loaded from google fonts.
music
"moog city", a slowed + reverb version. the original song is by C418.
pictures
the pencil-rifle is my own picture. the big faint rifle behind every page is my line drawing.
pages
when you click around, the page content gets swapped in with a little javascript instead of reloading, which is why the music doesn't restart.

// what it keeps on your device

only in your browser's own storage, and none of it is sent anywhere: music volume, loop and player size, the contrast and motion switches, your accent colour and text size, how many times you opened the home page, which changelog entries you've seen, which hidden things you found, the numbers on the stats page, and your draft message plus the ids of tickets you've sent.

the outside requests are google fonts, the projects page asking github's public api when a repo was last pushed, and the contact form (only when you send a ticket) going through a form service that emails it to me.
