AUTORESPONDER SETTINGS FOR WEB3FORMS  (the reply visitors get after sending a ticket)
=====================================================================================
important, from web3forms' own docs:
  - the autoresponder is a PAID (pro) feature.
  - it can only change 5 things (below). you can NOT give it your own html, so it can't be fully
    themed. the purple look is only possible in the email that comes to YOU (notification.html).
  - it only sends if the ticket has an "email" field. my forms add that field only when the visitor
    chose "email" as where to reply (or typed an email in the simple form).
  - it only works on the live site (donidonka.info), not on localhost.

where: app.web3forms.com -> your form -> settings -> autoresponder

1) From Name
   DoniDonka

2) Autoresponder Subject
   got your ticket {field:ticket_id}

3) Autoresponder Intro Text
   thanks for writing in. this is just to say your ticket got to me. your ticket id is {field:ticket_id}, so keep it if you need to mention it later. i read these myself, and i'll answer the way you asked me to.

4) Show a copy of their submission in the email
   Yes

5) Logo image (full https:// address, png)
   https://donidonka.info/assets/icon-512.png
   (this one is a rifle on a dark square, so it looks right on any email background. the transparent avatar would disappear on white.)

note on the wording: it doesn't promise how fast i'll answer, on purpose. add that yourself if you want to make that promise.
the {field:ticket_id} bits use web3forms' merge-tag format from their changelog. if your dashboard shows a different format for variables, use that one instead.
