/ FAQ

questions people might have.

things people ask about the site. type in the box to filter them.

does the music restart when i click around?

no. the pages swap in without a full reload, so the song keeps going. if you open a page in a new tab it does start fresh, because that's a brand new page.

why doesn't the music start by itself?

browsers block sound that starts without a click. press play on the player, or press k. after that it stays on across pages.

does this site track me?

no analytics, no ads, no cookies, no accounts. your settings live in your own browser. the privacy page lists exactly what is stored and what leaves your device.

how do i turn the animations off?

use the motion button in the header. if your device is set to reduce motion, the site follows that on its own.

how do i change the colours or the text size?

at the bottom of any page there are two small buttons, accent and text. accent cycles gray, purple, ice and green. text cycles 100, 115 and 130 percent. both are remembered on your device.

how do i report a bug?

use "report a problem" in the footer, or the full ticket form if you want to give more detail.

where is the code?

it's public on github: DoniDonka/officialwebsite. every page also has a "view source" link at the bottom that goes straight to its file.

can i use your picture or colours?

the brand page has the files and the colours. if you're not sure whether you can use something, send me a ticket and ask.

how do i get notified about updates?

there's a feed (the "feed" link in the footer), and the updates link in the nav gets a small dot when something new is posted since your last visit.
